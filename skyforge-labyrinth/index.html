You wake in a courtyard by a fountain. Four paths lead out into an endless overgrown labyrinth hiding castles, sky fortresses, dungeons, caves and winding tunnels. Every one ends in a treasure chest full of coins.

This is an exploring game: find treasure chests for coins and spend them in the shop on gear that helps you explore: torches (select one on your hotbar and it lights up around you for 3 minutes), the Stonebreaker Hammer (breaks anything, wears out after 300 blocks), a glider, spring boots, portal stones, and the legendary Magic Carpet. The floating castles in the sky have no way up: save your coins for the Magic Carpet.

No monsters, just exploring. Doorways are at least 2 wide and 3 tall, stairs climb one block at a time, slabs need no jump, and you can swim out of any water.

Two players: connect a second controller and press any button on it. The screen splits, one half per controller.
